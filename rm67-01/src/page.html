The sequence

    Start over

    The big idea: your wrist is the battery.

    In three movements

    1. 01

      Wrist → rotor

      A heavy platinum weight hangs off-centre. Your arm moves; the weight swings and turns.

    2. 02

      Rotor → spring

      Turning one way only (OneWay), it winds the mainspring through a row of small wheels.

    3. 03

      Spring → tick

      The spring lets go slowly. The balance swings 8 times a second, letting the gears move one notch at a time.

    Specifications

    Calibre

    CRMA6, skeletonised automatic winding. Hours, minutes, date and function indicator.

    • Dimensions29.10 × 31.25 mmRM
    • Thickness3.60 mmRM
    • Jewels25RM

    Power reserve

    About fifty hours when fully wound. The barrel drains one second per second; the bridle slips when full.

    • Reserve≈ 50 h (±10%)RM
    • Rotor turns, 0 → full1,000est.
    • Barrel turns, 0 → full6.5est.

    Regulation

    Free-sprung balance with variable inertia, adjusted by four small weights on the rim.

    • Frequency28,800 vph · 4 Hz · 8 ticks/sRM
    • Inertia7.5 mg·cm²RM
    • Lift angle50°RM
    • Escape wheel20 teethstd.
    • Amplitude280° → 170°std.

    Winding

    A platinum rotor on ceramic ball bearings, winding in one direction only through the OneWay system.

    • RotorPlatinum, OneWayRM
    • Per walking step0.12 turnsest.

    Function selector

    A hand at 2 o'clock shows the crown's job: W for winding, D for the date, H for setting the hands.

    • PositionsW · D · HRM
    • DateSemi-instantaneous, 5 o'clockRM

    Case

    Tonneau, three parts, screwed through with spline screws. Sapphire front and back.

    • Dimensions38.70 × 47.52 mmRM
    • Thickness7.75 mmRM

    Sources

    • RM: Richard Mille's published RM 67-01 / CRMA6 specification: 38.70 × 47.52 × 7.75 mm case, 29.10 × 31.25 × 3.60 mm movement, 28,800 vph, about 50 h (±10%), 25 jewels, CuBe balance at 7.5 mg·cm² with a 50° lift angle, platinum rotor with OneWay winding on ceramic ball bearings, function hand at 2 o'clock showing W · D · H, semi-instantaneous date in a vertical aperture at 5 o'clock, grade 5 titanium spline screws.
    • Corrected from the brief: the selector reads W · D · H (there is no N). RM publishes no variable-geometry rotor or adjustable ribs for the CRMA6 (that idea belongs to other RM calibers), so the rib step and slider became OneWay against a both-ways what-if. The RM 67-01 has no seconds hand; the fourth wheel, which turns once a minute, carries a marker dot instead.
    • std.: 20-tooth escape wheel and the 280° → 170° amplitude curve are typical of a Swiss lever movement; RM doesn't publish them for the CRMA6.
    • est.: 1,000 rotor turns for a full wind, 6.5 barrel turns, 0.12 winding turns per step, 16 h a day on the wrist, each activity's steps and extra wrist-motion turns (golf 12,000 steps + 60 turns from swings, skiing 6,000 + 250, tennis 9,000 + 200, sailing 4,000 + 250, racing cars 3,000 + 40), and the both-ways × 1.6 factor are estimates chosen to be plausible. They set how fast things wind, not the timekeeping.
    • Drawn, not measured: the layout of the wheels in the movement, the number of bezel screws, and the strap and dial details follow the watch's look, not a technical drawing.
